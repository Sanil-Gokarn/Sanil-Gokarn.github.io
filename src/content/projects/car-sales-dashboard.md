## Overview

A single view of automotive sales, customer preferences, dealer performance and market trends, built so managers can track growth and spot where to act.

## What I did

- **Dashboard development.** 6 interactive pages with 20+ KPIs covering revenue, sales performance, customer preferences, dealer performance and budget vs forecast.
- **Data preparation.** Cleaned, transformed and validated 100,000+ records with Excel and Power Query.
- **Statistical analysis.** Ran correlation and regression analysis in Excel to understand what drives sales.
- **BI modelling.** DAX measures, KPI cards, trend visuals and slicer-based views for management reporting.

## Key insights

- **SUVs (28%) and hatchbacks (23%)** lead body-style preference, so dealers should prioritise stocking them.
- **Repeat buyers fell 7.85% year to date** while churn rose, which points to a need for loyalty and trade-in offers.
- **Average price slipped 0.79% year on year** despite sales growth, a risk to margins worth addressing with bundles rather than price cuts.
- **Sales spike from September to December**, so inventory and staffing should ramp up ahead of that window.
- **Automatic transmissions make up 52%** of sales and are trending up.
