## Overview

Most people who need business numbers don't know how to navigate a dashboard. InsFlow lets them ask questions in plain English and get answers backed by governed, auditable data.

## What I built

- **Conversational reporting assistant.** In controlled benchmarking against traditional reporting methods, it cut the time to retrieve a metric by 90%.
- **Data integration.** Connected 5+ business KPIs through Microsoft Fabric Lakehouses, so natural language questions run against structured, governed reporting data.
- **AI-assisted operational analysis.** Applied AI to 500+ operational tickets to find trends and context that numbers alone don't show.
- **Governance and reliability.** Added source traceability and query validation, so every AI-generated answer is consistent, accurate and auditable.

## Impact

A self-service reporting tool that opens business intelligence up to people without dashboard expertise, while keeping the same reporting standards as the official numbers.
