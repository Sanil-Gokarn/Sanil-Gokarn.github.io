## Overview

A consulting-style project for HS2 on improving Birmingham's railway stations, focused on accessibility, passenger flow, efficiency and passenger experience.

## What I did

- **Research and discovery.** Led field research across 3 Birmingham stations, assessing infrastructure, accessibility, passenger experience and operations.
- **Business analysis.** Identified improvements in efficiency, sustainability and passenger experience, and turned them into practical recommendations.
- **Leadership.** Led a 5-person team through research planning, analysis, recommendations and the final presentation to board and C-level HS2 stakeholders.

## Impact

Recognised in the **Top 5 out of 60 teams**, showing leadership, stakeholder-facing analysis and clear communication of solutions.
