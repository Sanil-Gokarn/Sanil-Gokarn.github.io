## Overview

Public road safety data is detailed but hard to read. This project turns it into a clear view of where accidents happen, how severe they are and what contributes to them.

## What I did

- **Data preparation and modelling.** Cleaned, validated and transformed 100,000+ public records in SQL/MySQL, with missing value checks, joins, analytical views and severity-based metrics.
- **Statistical analysis.** Correlation and regression analysis on location, road conditions and severity.
- **BI reporting.** Power BI data models, relationships, DAX measures and dashboard views for accident trends, risk hotspots and severity distribution.

## Impact

Turns a complex public dataset into structured visual insights that support evidence-based road safety reviews.
