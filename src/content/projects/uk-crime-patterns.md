## Overview

Police records and the Crime Survey for England and Wales often tell different stories. This study compares the two over ten years to see which trends are real and where crime goes unreported.

## What I did

- **Data preparation and validation.** Cleaned and transformed 13+ ONS Police Recorded Crime and CSEW datasets into consistent outputs for long-term trend analysis.
- **Comparative analysis** of 5 major categories, including theft, fraud and violent crime, to find proportional shifts and structural changes.
- **Statistical analysis.** Correlation and comparative statistics to identify reporting differences, underreporting patterns and variation between sources.

## Impact

Evidence-based findings on how reliable crime trends are and what they say about public safety.
