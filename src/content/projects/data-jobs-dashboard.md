## Overview

Information about the data job market is scattered. This dashboard puts 2024's data job postings into one easy view for job seekers and people switching careers.

## What I built

- **Market overview page** with KPI cards (479K jobs, $113K median yearly salary, $47.62 median hourly wage), monthly posting trends, top-paying roles and a role comparison table.
- **Drill-through page** for any job title, with salary gauges, the share of remote and no-degree roles, health insurance coverage, a global map and a breakdown by job platform.
- **Interactivity** through slicers, buttons, bookmarks and drill-through.

## Skills shown

- ETL with Power Query: handling blanks, fixing data types and adding calculated columns.
- DAX measures for median salaries and job counts.
- Geospatial analysis with map visuals.
- Dashboard design that tells a clear data story.
